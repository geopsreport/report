As the second anniversary of the October 7 Gaza conflict approaches, the global geopolitical landscape is marked by intensifying scrutiny of Israeli actions, mounting public dissent in the West, and deepening fractures within the international order. The past 12 hours have brought new revelations about covert operations, diplomatic maneuvering, and the human cost of ongoing crises.

**Mossad’s Historical Role in Italian Political Violence Resurfaces**

A major exposé by **Max Blumenthal** has reignited debate over Western intelligence operations in Europe, revealing that Israel’s Mossad maintained contact “from the very beginning” with the faction responsible for the 1978 kidnapping and murder of Italian Prime Minister Aldo Moro. This revelation, based on testimony from a veteran journalist, suggests a troubling depth of Israeli involvement in Cold War-era political violence and underscores Italy’s transformation into what Blumenthal calls a “US-Israeli aircraft carrier.” The timing of these disclosures, amid Italy’s current wave of pro-Palestinian activism, is fueling renewed skepticism about the extent of foreign influence on European politics.

**Flotilla Crackdown: Reports of Abuse and Drone Strikes on Activists**

Fresh details have emerged regarding Israel’s recent raid on the Global Sumud Flotilla. According to **Caitlin Johnstone**, Israeli forces not only detained hundreds of international volunteers—including Greta Thunberg—but also deployed drones against the flotilla. Thunberg and other detainees reportedly endured “dehydration and bedbug infestations” while attempting to deliver formula to starving infants in Gaza. These accounts are intensifying criticism of Israel’s blockade tactics and raising questions about the treatment of high-profile activists in custody.

**Ukraine War: Stalemate and Western Strategic Uncertainty**

On the Eastern front, the Ukraine conflict remains locked in a costly stalemate. **Larry C. Johnson** highlights insights from Trump administration sources, as reported by Sy Hersh, indicating that President Trump has been “bamboozled” about Ukraine’s prospects. The article critiques the continued influence of figures like General David Petraeus and points to growing frustration within the U.S. administration over the lack of progress. This reflects a broader reassessment of Western interventionism, with analysts noting that Washington is increasingly aware of the limits of its military and diplomatic leverage.

**Trends and Outlook**

The majority of analysts agree that the credibility of Western institutions is under sustained pressure, both from the exposure of past covert operations and from the visible disconnect between official policy and public sentiment. The harsh treatment of activists and the resurfacing of Cold War-era intelligence scandals are fueling distrust and galvanizing protest movements, particularly in Europe. Meanwhile, the Ukraine war’s protracted stalemate and the Trump administration’s internal divisions signal a period of strategic drift for the West. In the coming days, expect continued revelations about intelligence activities, heightened scrutiny of Israel’s conduct, and growing calls for accountability from Western governments.
