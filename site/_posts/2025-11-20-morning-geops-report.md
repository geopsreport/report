As the global order remains unsettled by persistent conflict and shifting alliances, the past 12 hours have brought new insights into covert diplomacy in Ukraine, the orchestration of unrest in Mexico, and renewed scrutiny of US-Saudi relations—each with significant implications for regional stability and international power dynamics.

**Ukraine: Secret Peace Plan Revealed Amid Ongoing Stalemate**

Fresh details have surfaced regarding clandestine efforts to end the Ukraine war. **Gilbert Doctorow** reports that a secret 28-point peace plan was developed through direct collaboration between representatives from the Trump and Putin camps, intended for delivery to Ukrainian President Zelensky. The plan’s handover, scheduled for Istanbul, was abruptly canceled, highlighting both the fragility and secrecy of these negotiations. Doctorow notes that this covert diplomacy “underscores the ongoing search for a negotiated settlement,” but skepticism remains high among analysts, who point to entrenched positions and a lack of consensus among key stakeholders. Meanwhile, as battlefield realities continue to shift—with Russian advances and Ukrainian attrition—analysts warn that the absence of a transparent, inclusive process may limit prospects for a durable peace.

**Mexico: Protests Exposed as Elite-Backed Operation**

The narrative of mass protests in Mexico City has shifted dramatically. What was initially portrayed as a Gen-Z uprising against government inaction on crime has now been revealed as a coordinated campaign backed by local oligarchs and international right-wing networks. **Max Blumenthal** details how these demonstrations, which escalated into violence after the assassination of a local mayor, were orchestrated to undermine President Claudia Sheinbaum. This revelation complicates the narrative of grassroots dissent and, according to the majority of analysts, signals a broader trend of elite and foreign interference in Latin American politics.

**US-Saudi Relations: Calls for Transparency Intensify**

Tensions in US-Saudi relations have resurfaced, with new demands for transparency. **Chris Hedges** highlights Representative Eugene Vindman’s call for President Trump to release the transcript of a sensitive phone call with the Saudi Crown Prince. Representative Ilhan Omar has echoed these concerns, emphasizing the need for accountability in light of ongoing human rights abuses and the administration’s pursuit of deeper security and economic ties with Riyadh. The majority opinion among analysts is that these opaque dealings risk further eroding US credibility and complicating its alliances in the region.

**Trends and Outlook**

The dominant trend is one of unresolved crises managed through backchannel negotiations and elite maneuvering, rather than open or sustainable solutions. In Ukraine, secret diplomacy reflects a reluctance to pursue transparent peace efforts, while in Mexico, the exposure of orchestrated unrest points to increasing manipulation of civil movements. US foreign policy continues to face scrutiny over its alliances with authoritarian partners. Analysts broadly expect continued volatility, with further revelations and potential flashpoints likely as underlying tensions remain unaddressed.
