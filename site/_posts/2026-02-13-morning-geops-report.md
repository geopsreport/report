**U.S.-Iran Military Calculus: Limits of Air Power and Missile Threats**

Johnson notes that while F-35A and F-35C stealth fighters would likely be central to any campaign, “Iran’s missile capabilities and support for regional groups remain significant obstacles to any successful military action.” The assessment underscores that Iran’s robust air defenses and the risk of asymmetric retaliation by allied militias complicate U.S. military options, making a decisive strike far from assured. This comes as the U.S. continues to reinforce its naval presence in the region, yet refrains from direct action—reflecting a strategic pause amid high risks.
